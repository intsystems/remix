\section{Introduction}
Fitting a finite mixture requires learning where its components place mass
and how often each is sampled. Differentiating a Gaussian draw with respect
to its mean accounts for the first effect. Learning its weight also requires
accounting for the categorical choice that produced the draw. ReMix lets a
PyTorch user change how both derivatives are estimated within one training loop.

Stratification handles the categorical choice by evaluating every component
\citep{morningstar2021mixtures}. Implicit reparameterization obtains a
derivative of a single mixture draw from its distribution function
\citep{figurnov2018implicit}. \citet{burroni2026mixture} relate these methods
to post-stratification, which averages component derivatives at one sampled
point.
