\section{Problem Setup}
Consider $K$ continuous component densities $q_k(z;\phi_k)$ on
$\mathbb R^d$, with component parameters $\phi_k$. Let $\alpha$ be a vector
of $K$ logits, $\pi=\operatorname{softmax}(\alpha)$ their mixture weights,
and $\theta=(\alpha,\phi_1,\ldots,\phi_K)$. To sample a point $Z$, draw a
component label $C$ and then draw from its density. Writing $q_k$ for
$q_k(\,\cdot\,;\phi_k)$ gives
\begin{equation}
 C\sim\operatorname{Categorical}(\pi),\qquad
 Z\mid C=k\sim q_k,\qquad
 q_\theta(z)=\sum_{k=1}^K\pi_k q_k(z).
 \label{eq:mixture}
\end{equation}
Given the point $z$, its \emph{responsibility}
$r_k(z)=\pi_k q_k(z)/q_\theta(z)$ is the conditional probability that $C=k$.
When component $k$ is reparameterizable, we can generate its draw as
$Z_k=T_k(\varepsilon_k;\phi_k)$ from noise $\varepsilon_k$ whose law is
independent of $\theta$.

Assume common fixed support and that we may differentiate under the
expectations below. For a scalar objective $f_\theta(z)$, we seek an unbiased
estimate of
\begin{equation}
 \nabla_\theta \mathcal J(\theta),\qquad
 \mathcal J(\theta)=\mathbb E_{Z\sim q_\theta}[f_\theta(Z)]
       =\sum_{k=1}^K\pi_k\,\mathbb E_{Z_k\sim q_k}[f_\theta(Z_k)].
 \label{eq:objective}
\end{equation}
