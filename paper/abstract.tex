We specify four unbiased estimators of gradients of mixture expectations:
score-function, stratified, implicit reparameterization, and post-stratified
estimators. A common formulation separates mixture-weight derivatives, component
transport, and explicit objective dependence. For the evidence lower bound, we
state the Sticking the Landing modification and give first-order
automatic-differentiation constructions, applicability conditions, and evaluation
costs.
