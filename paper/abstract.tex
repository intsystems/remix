Finite mixture distributions provide flexible posterior approximations whose
optimization requires gradients through both component parameters and mixture
weights. We review the main approaches to this problem and describe four
unbiased estimators: score-function, stratified, implicit reparameterization,
and post-stratified. A running example connects their sampling procedures and
gradients, and a comparison explains their variance and computational tradeoffs,
including the Sticking the Landing modification for variational inference.
These estimators will form ReMix, a PyTorch library that separates mixture
distributions, objectives, and gradient estimation so that methods can be
exchanged within the same optimization loop.
