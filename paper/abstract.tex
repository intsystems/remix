ReMix is a PyTorch library for optimizing finite mixtures. We compare the
sampling cost and parameter updates of four unbiased gradient estimators.
Stratification evaluates the objective in every component. For a quadratic
objective on a two-Gaussian mixture, the post-stratified update at one draw
matches the implicit update for the means and the zero-baseline score-function
update for the weights. We demonstrate the shared training interface at a
fixed budget of objective evaluations.
