Finite mixture distributions provide flexible posterior approximations whose
optimization requires gradients through both component parameters and mixture
weights. We review the main approaches to this problem and describe four
unbiased estimators: score-function, stratified, implicit reparameterization,
and post-stratified. For each method, we explain its construction, requirements,
and computational cost, including the Sticking the Landing modification for
variational inference. These estimators will form ReMix, a PyTorch library with
a shared interface for optimization and comparison.
